Qariah is the first dedicated Qur'an audio app curated entirely from women reciters — a quiet, ad-free space for Muslim women, families, and learners who want to hear the Qur'an in voices like their own.

WHY QARIAH

• A library only of women reciters. Every recording in Qariah is by a trained female qariah, and the catalogue grows release after release.

• Multiple riwāyāt. Listen across Hafs an Asim, Warsh an Nafi', Qālūn an Nafi', and Sūsī an Abī ʿAmr — note the differences, feel each tradition.

• Truly ad-free. No banners, no interstitials, ever.

• Designed for women. Soft, modest, calm. No imagery of public performance, no marketing-driven UI — just the Qur'an.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

• A growing roster of women reciters, with full surahs in their primary riwāyah.

• Crisp audio streamed from a private CDN — works on slow connections.

• Background playback, lock-screen controls, and continue-where-you-left-off.

• Bookmark your favorite reciters and surahs.

WHO IT'S FOR

• Muslim women looking to listen to recitation in voices that feel familiar.

• Mothers and teachers introducing daughters to recitation and tajwīd.

• Anyone who wants to broaden their listening beyond the male reciters who dominate every other Qur'an app.

PRIVACY

No ads, ever. We don't sell your data and we don't use advertising IDs.

You can listen without an account. Signing in is optional: it uses your Quran Foundation account, keeps your private notes with you across devices, and lets you publish reflections to QuranReflect.

Qariah sends anonymous usage analytics (you can turn them off in Settings → Privacy) and crash reports, so we can improve the app and fix bugs. Notifications are optional, and you choose which ones you get.

Full privacy policy: https://qariah.org/privacy

CONTACT

Questions, feedback, or a recording you'd like us to consider? Email info@qariah.org.

Qariah is a sister project of Bayaan (an open-source Qur'an app for male reciters) and inherits the same audio engine and design discipline.
